Blogs and essays I have found genuinely useful for doing better research. Sharing them here in case they help someone else. Listed in chronological order of publication, oldest first.

Principles of Effective Research
Michael Nielsen, July 2004
On the difference between working hard and working on the right things, and on building the long-term habits that compound into good research.
The Bitter Lesson
Richard Sutton, March 2019
A short essay arguing that general methods which leverage computation have repeatedly won out over approaches that build in human knowledge.
An Opinionated Guide to ML Research
John Schulman, January 2020
Practical advice on choosing problems worth working on and on the day-to-day habits that turn open-ended research into steady progress.
